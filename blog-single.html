ChemDist Heavy Duty Solvent Degreaser (SD-400)

Published: Jan 2026 · CAS # 64742-89-8 · CAS / GHS Compliant

ChemDist Heavy Duty Solvent Degreaser (SD-400) is a high-potency, fast-evaporating industrial cleaning formula engineered for rapid removal of heavy petroleum oils, grease, tar, and manufacturing residues from metal surfaces, dip tanks, and automated parts washers.

1. Identification & Product Hazard Classification

Classified under GHS HazCom 2012 standards. Flammable Liquid Category 3, Skin Irritation Category 2, Specific Target Organ Toxicity (Single Exposure) Category 3.

  • Signal Word: DANGER
  • Hazard Statements: Flammable liquid and vapor. Causes skin irritation. May cause drowsiness or dizziness.
  • Precautionary Statements: Keep away from heat, sparks, open flames, and hot surfaces. Wear nitrile chemical-resistant gloves and splash-proof goggles.

2. Physical & Chemical Properties

  • Boiling Point: 155°C - 175°C (311°F - 347°F)
  • Flash Point: 42°C (107.6°F) Closed Cup
  • Specific Gravity: 0.79 at 20°C
  • Solubility in Water: Negligible (< 0.1%)

3. Handling, Storage & Bulk Container Options

Store in original grounded steel drums or IBC totes in a cool, well-ventilated dedicated HazMat chemical storage facility. Available in 5-gallon pails, 55-gallon closed-head steel drums, and 275-gallon tote containers.
