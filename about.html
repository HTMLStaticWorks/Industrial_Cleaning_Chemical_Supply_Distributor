Milestones

Our journey of growth & excellence

From a local solvent terminal to an enterprise chemical logistics partner serving 1,500+ commercial clients nationwide.

2008

Founding of ChemDist Pro

Established our initial 25,000 sq ft regional solvent packaging facility in Ohio with 3 dedicated delivery trucks serving local metalworking and automotive manufacturers.

2013

Automated Bulk Drum & Tote Filling Lines

Installed high-speed automated filling lines for 55-gallon drums and 275-gallon IBC totes with nitrogen-purged tanks for sensitive solvent formulations.

2017

ISO 9001:2015 & EPA Registration

Attained ISO 9001:2015 certification for our analytical quality assurance laboratory and registered our hospital-grade disinfectant production with the EPA.

2021

Digital Client Portal & Telemetry Replenishment

Launched our proprietary B2B client dashboard, providing live SDS downloads, digital invoices, and IoT tank-level automated replenishment alerts.

2026

Nationwide Reach with 1,500+ Accounts

Today, ChemDist Pro operates over 180,000 sq ft of distribution terminal space, managing 500+ SKUs with a 99.4% on-time delivery record across all commercial accounts.
